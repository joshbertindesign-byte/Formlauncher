Colour means a kind of data

A form takes the colour of the structure it mainly saves to, so a person learns "green is the run, amber is stock" once and it holds everywhere.

For the floor

Operator screens use 48 to 56 px targets, 17 px type for what is entered, and plain words. Structure, container and entry stay in the editor.

Warm, not soft

Neutrals lean slightly warm. Contrast stays high, corners stay modest, and there are no gradients, illustrations or pastels.
